# LLL Algebra

Developed by LLL Inc.'s dev team — visit us at https://www.live-laugh-love.world

A free, gamified algebra equation solver. Pick the correct operation to isolate
x, one step at a time, with instant feedback — no account required, progress
saved locally in your browser.

**Live:** https://lll-algebra.pages.dev/

## Features

- **Step-by-step solving** — tap the correct operation to isolate x; wrong choices shake, correct ones advance
- **Staircase-climb visual** — a chalkboard-styled staircase that fills as you solve each step, sized to match the equation's difficulty
- **Difficulty tiers** — Easy, Medium, and Hard (including "x on both sides" equations)
- **Streak bonus** — bonus points for consecutive no-hint solves
- **Achievements** — unlockable achievements with toast notifications and a browsable panel
- **Daily challenge** — a new equation each day, with a streak counter for consecutive days
- **Onboarding walkthrough** — animated intro demo for first-time visitors
- **Progress saved locally** — score, level, and streaks persist across sessions
- **Light/dark theme** and English/Japanese language support
